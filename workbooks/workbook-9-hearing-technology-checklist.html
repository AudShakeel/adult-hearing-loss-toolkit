Part 5: Before I buy a device

Before buying, I will ask:

  • ☐ What problem does this solve?
  • ☐ Does it work with my hearing aids?
  • ☐ Does it work with my phone or TV?
  • ☐ Is it easy for me to use?
  • ☐ Does it need charging?
  • ☐ Does it need an app?

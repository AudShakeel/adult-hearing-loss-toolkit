What I want others to understand

  • ☐ I can hear speech but do not always understand the words
  • ☐ I hear better when I can see the speaker's face
  • ☐ Background noise makes listening much harder
  • ☐ I miss more when people speak from another room
  • ☐ Shouting does not always help
  • ☐ Rephrasing helps more than repeating the same words
  • ☐ Written information helps me remember important details
  • ☐ I want to be included

What helps me most

  • ☐ Say my name before speaking
  • ☐ Face me when speaking
  • ☐ Move closer
  • ☐ Speak clearly
  • ☐ Do not shout
  • ☐ Do not speak from another room
  • ☐ Reduce background noise
  • ☐ Rephrase when I miss something
  • ☐ Speak one at a time
  • ☐ Write down important details

Workbook Activity: My Hearing Challenges Checklist

Check any situations where you notice difficulty hearing or understanding.

Conversations

  • ☐ One-on-one conversations
  • ☐ Group conversations
  • ☐ Family gatherings
  • ☐ Conversations with children
  • ☐ Conversations with soft-spoken people
  • ☐ Conversations when someone speaks from another room
  • ☐ Conversations when the speaker is not facing me

Background noise

  • ☐ Restaurants
  • ☐ Coffee shops
  • ☐ Cars
  • ☐ Work meetings
  • ☐ Social events
  • ☐ Religious or community gatherings
  • ☐ Stores or public places

Technology and media

  • ☐ Phone calls
  • ☐ Video calls
  • ☐ Television
  • ☐ Movies
  • ☐ Music
  • ☐ Public announcements
  • ☐ Alarms, timers, or doorbells

Emotional or daily-life impact

  • ☐ I feel tired after listening for a long time
  • ☐ I avoid certain social situations
  • ☐ I feel frustrated when I miss words
  • ☐ I worry that others think I am not listening
  • ☐ I ask people to repeat often
  • ☐ I pretend to understand when I do not
  • ☐ I feel less confident in conversations

Key Takeaway

Hearing loss is not just about making sounds louder. It can affect speech clarity, listening effort, confidence, relationships, safety, and daily participation. Understanding your hearing loss is the first step toward managing it well. With the right support, many adults with hearing loss can communicate more confidently and stay connected to the people and activities that matter most.

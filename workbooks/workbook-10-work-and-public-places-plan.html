Part 5: My appointment plan

Before medical or professional appointments, I will:

  • ☐ Tell the office I have hearing loss
  • ☐ Bring hearing aids or assistive technology
  • ☐ Bring a communication partner if desired
  • ☐ Ask for written instructions
  • ☐ Prepare questions in advance
  • ☐ Ask for a visit summary
  • ☐ Use the patient portal for follow-up

During the appointment, I will:

  • ☐ Ask the provider to face me
  • ☐ Ask for rephrasing when needed
  • ☐ Repeat back important information
  • ☐ Ask for names, numbers, or instructions in writing
  • ☐ Ask for clarification before leaving

Part 7: My self-advocacy scripts

Practice these phrases.

  • ☐ “I have hearing loss. Please face me when you speak.”
  • ☐ “Could you write that down?”
  • ☐ “Could you send that by email?”
  • ☐ “Could you turn on captions?”
  • ☐ “Can we move away from the noise?”
  • ☐ “Please speak one at a time.”
  • ☐ “I missed the last part. Could you rephrase it?”
  • ☐ “Let me repeat that back to make sure I understood.”
  • ☐ “Could you put the date and time in the chat?”
  • ☐ “Is an assistive listening system available?”

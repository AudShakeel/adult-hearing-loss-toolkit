Educational Hearing Check

10-Question Hearing Check

This tool does not diagnose hearing loss. It helps adults decide whether a comprehensive audiological evaluation may be a good next step.

Before you start

If you have sudden hearing loss, sudden ringing or buzzing, ear pain, ear drainage, dizziness, vertigo, sudden ear pressure, or new one-sided hearing trouble, seek medical care promptly. Do not use a website quiz as your next step.

Answer yes or no. Your result gives a recommendation, not a diagnosis. Your answers stay on your device.

Why audiologist first?

A full evaluation gives you the hearing map

A licensed audiologist can evaluate the type, degree, and pattern of hearing loss, speech understanding, middle ear function, and whether medical referral is needed.

A hearing aid dispenser or retail hearing center may help with devices later, but buying a device is not the same as receiving a diagnostic audiological evaluation.

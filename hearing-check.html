10-Question Hearing Check

This tool is educational. It does not diagnose hearing loss. Your answers stay on your device.

Before you start

If you have sudden hearing loss, sudden ringing or buzzing, ear pain, ear drainage, dizziness, vertigo, sudden ear pressure, or new one-sided hearing trouble, seek medical care promptly. Do not use a website quiz as your next step.

Answer yes or no. The result tells you whether a comprehensive audiological evaluation may be a good next step.

Why this tool recommends an audiologist

A licensed audiologist can evaluate the type, degree, and pattern of hearing loss, speech understanding, and need for medical referral. A hearing aid dispenser or retail hearing center may help with devices later, but a device purchase is not the same as a diagnostic evaluation.

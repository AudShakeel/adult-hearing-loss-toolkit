Start here

Hearing loss is not a personal failure. It is a listening access problem. Small changes in the room, the speaker, the tools, and the plan can make communication easier.

BRIGHT steps

Bring the voice closer, Reduce noise, Include a partner, Get the right tool, Have a repair phrase, Test with an audiologist.

Audiologist first

A full audiology evaluation is the sound map. Buying a hearing aid is not the same as diagnosis.

Urgent signs

Sudden hearing loss, sudden ringing, ear pain, drainage, dizziness, or new one-sided symptoms need prompt medical care.

10-question hearing check

Should I see an audiologist?

This educational tool cannot diagnose hearing loss. It can help you decide whether a comprehensive hearing evaluation with an audiologist may be helpful.

Seek urgent medical care

Do not use this tool for sudden hearing loss, ear pain, ear drainage, dizziness, sudden ringing, or sudden changes in hearing. Those symptoms need prompt medical attention.

Why an audiologist?

An audiologist provides a comprehensive hearing evaluation, explains the type and degree of hearing loss, checks speech understanding, looks for referral concerns, and helps connect results to real-life communication needs. Buying a device is not the same as receiving a complete audiologic evaluation.

What happens next?

If the tool suggests an evaluation, consider scheduling a comprehensive hearing evaluation with an audiologist. An audiologist can explain your hearing results, discuss medical referral concerns when needed, and recommend options based on your hearing, communication needs, and goals.

When not to wait

Seek urgent medical care for sudden hearing loss, ear pain, drainage, dizziness, sudden ringing, or sudden changes in hearing.

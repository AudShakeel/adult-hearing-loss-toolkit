Project Development Process

  • Identified common adult aural rehabilitation counseling needs.
  • Selected 12 common patient-facing topics.
  • Organized topics into four patient-journey categories.
  • Used evidence-based sources only.
  • Created workbook activities for carryover outside the appointment.
  • Built view, print, and download functionality.
  • Added patient, family, and clinician pathways.

Connection to Adult Aural Rehabilitation

This toolkit supports adult aural rehabilitation by combining education, counseling, strategy training, technology orientation, and long-term planning. It is designed to support:

  • Informational counseling
  • Personal adjustment counseling
  • Communication strategy training
  • Hearing aid orientation
  • Assistive technology education
  • Communication partner training
  • Self-advocacy
  • Participation and quality of life
  • Long-term hearing care planning

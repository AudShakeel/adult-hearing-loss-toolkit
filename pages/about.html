About this toolkit

This page describes the project purpose, contributors, development process, and connection to adult aural rehabilitation.

Project overview

Hear Better, Live Brighter: An Adult Aural Rehabilitation Toolkit was developed as part of an academic adult aural rehabilitation project focused on evidence-based patient education, counseling support, communication strategy training, and hearing health resource development.

This toolkit was developed by AuD candidates at the University of the Pacific in San Francisco, California.

Project Development Process

  • Identified common adult aural rehabilitation counseling needs.
  • Selected 12 common patient-facing topics.
  • Organized topics into four patient-journey categories.
  • Used evidence-based sources only.
  • Created workbook activities for carryover outside the appointment.
  • Built view, print, and download functionality.
  • Added patient, family, and clinician pathways.

Connection to Adult Aural Rehabilitation

This toolkit supports adult aural rehabilitation by combining education, counseling, strategy training, technology orientation, and long-term planning. It is designed to support:

  • Informational counseling
  • Personal adjustment counseling
  • Communication strategy training
  • Hearing aid orientation
  • Assistive technology education
  • Communication partner training
  • Self-advocacy
  • Participation and quality of life
  • Long-term hearing care planning

Evidence base

The toolkit uses professional, public health, government, clinical guideline, systematic review, and peer-reviewed sources, including ASHA, NIDCD/NIH, the American Academy of Audiology, ADA.gov, Job Accommodation Network, Cochrane reviews, and peer-reviewed audiology and public health literature.

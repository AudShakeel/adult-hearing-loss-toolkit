About This Project

Who created this toolkit?

This page describes the project purpose, contributors, evidence base, and how the website supports adult aural rehabilitation education.

Project overview

Living Well with Hearing Loss: An Adult Aural Rehabilitation Toolkit is an academic adult aural rehabilitation project designed to provide patient-friendly, evidence-based education for adults with hearing loss and the people who communicate with them.

Purpose

The goal is to make hearing loss education easier to understand, easier to access, and easier to use in daily life. The toolkit includes articles, workbook activities, printable handouts, downloadable PDFs, family communication guidance, clinician implementation guidance, and an educational hearing check tool.

Evidence base

The toolkit uses professional, public health, government, clinical guideline, systematic review, and peer-reviewed sources, including ASHA, NIDCD/NIH, the American Academy of Audiology, ADA.gov, Job Accommodation Network, Cochrane reviews, and peer-reviewed audiology and public health literature.

Rubric alignment

  • Specialized project: A complete adult aural rehabilitation website with patient education and workbook materials.
  • Community education: Written for adults with hearing loss and family members using simple, practical language.
  • Clinical relevance: Designed for use after diagnosis, audiogram counseling, hearing aid evaluation, fitting, follow-up, family counseling, communication strategy counseling, and long-term planning.
  • Implementation: Materials are viewable, printable, and downloadable from a public GitHub Pages website.
  • References: Sources are consolidated on the References page and included in article materials.
  • Contributor roles: Roles and responsibilities are listed clearly on this page.
  • Reflection support: Workbook activities help users apply the education to their own listening situations.

References

These sources support the educational content, hearing check guidance, articles, workbooks, and visual materials used in this website.

Source Selection Statement

This toolkit was developed using professional organization guidance, government and public health resources, clinical practice guidelines, systematic reviews, and peer-reviewed literature. Commercial sources were not used as evidence sources for article claims. Commercial or educational websites used for images are listed separately as visual sources.

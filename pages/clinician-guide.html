For Clinicians

Using this toolkit in adult aural rehabilitation

This toolkit is designed for counseling, education, homework, follow-up planning, and family communication support.

When to use the toolkit

  • After diagnosis
  • During audiogram counseling
  • During hearing aid evaluation
  • After hearing aid fitting
  • During follow-up appointments
  • For communication strategy counseling
  • For family counseling
  • For assistive technology counseling
  • For emotional adjustment counseling
  • For long-term care planning

Suggested workflow

  1. Choose one article matched to the patient's concern.
  2. Print or send the paired workbook.
  3. Ask the patient to identify one listening goal.
  4. Review workbook responses at follow-up.
  5. Update the hearing care plan based on daily-life needs.

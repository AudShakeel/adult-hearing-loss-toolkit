Evidence Map

This table links toolkit topics to key sources and explains how the evidence was used.

Toolkit topicKey sourcesHow evidence was used
Hearing loss educationNIDCD; ASHAUsed to explain common adult hearing loss patterns, types of hearing loss, and when hearing care is appropriate.
Audiogram counselingASHA; British Society of Audiology; Salmon & FrankUsed to explain thresholds, frequency, loudness, air conduction, bone conduction, and interpretation basics.
Hearing aidsAmerican Academy of Audiology; ASHA; Ferguson et al.Used to explain realistic hearing aid expectations, fitting, counseling, verification, validation, and follow-up.
Communication strategiesASHA; American Academy of Audiology; Ferguson et al.Used to develop practical strategies for reducing communication breakdowns and improving daily-life listening.
Family supportKamil & Lin; ASHAUsed to support communication partner involvement and family-centered counseling.
Background noiseFerguson et al.; Maidment et al.; NIDCDUsed to explain why noise is difficult and when assistive listening technology may help.
Assistive technologyNIDCD; Maidment et al.; ASHAUsed to describe captions, remote microphones, TV tools, phone tools, alerting systems, and public listening systems.
Workplace and public accessADA.gov; Job Accommodation Network; ASHAUsed to explain access, effective communication, accommodations, and self-advocacy.
Emotional adjustmentShukla et al.; Lawrence et al.; Livingston et al.Used to discuss loneliness, social isolation, emotional well-being, and balanced counseling around cognition and hearing loss.
Long-term care planningAAA; ASHA; NIDCDUsed to support monitoring, follow-up, communication planning, technology review, and patient-centered goals.

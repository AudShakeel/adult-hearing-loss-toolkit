Educational use and accessibility

Disclaimer

This toolkit is for educational purposes only and does not replace individualized care, diagnosis, or treatment from an audiologist, physician, or other qualified healthcare professional. Seek urgent medical care for sudden hearing loss, ear pain, drainage, dizziness, or sudden changes in hearing.

Accessibility statement

All major materials are available as online pages, printable pages, and downloadable PDFs. The site uses readable text, clear headings, keyboard-friendly links, and print-friendly formatting.

Medical warning signs

  • Sudden hearing loss
  • Ear pain or drainage
  • Dizziness or vertigo
  • Sudden ringing or buzzing
  • Sudden change in hearing
  • New one-sided hearing symptoms

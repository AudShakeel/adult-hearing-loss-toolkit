For Family & Friends

How to support clearer communication

Family and friends can reduce communication barriers with small, respectful changes.

What hearing loss may feel like

Hearing loss can make conversation feel tiring, uncertain, or frustrating. A person may hear that someone is talking but miss the details needed to understand the message.

Why "I can hear you but cannot understand you" happens

Speech clarity depends on access to soft high-frequency speech sounds, distance, background noise, visual cues, room acoustics, and listening fatigue. Louder speech is not always clearer speech.

What not to do

  • Do not speak from another room.
  • Do not turn away while talking.
  • Do not shout.
  • Do not repeat the same unclear words over and over.
  • Do not say "never mind."
  • Do not assume hearing aids fix every situation.

What helps

  • Get attention first.
  • Face the listener.
  • Speak clearly.
  • Move closer.
  • Reduce background noise.
  • Rephrase when needed.
  • Speak one at a time.
  • Write down important details.
  • Be patient and respectful.

Family Communication Agreement

As a family or communication team, choose two habits to practice this week.

  • We will face each other before speaking.
  • We will not start important conversations from another room.
  • We will reduce background noise before important conversations.
  • We will rephrase instead of saying "never mind."
  • We will write down important details when needed.

For Family & Friends

How to support clearer communication

Family and friends can reduce communication barriers with small, respectful changes.

Helpful habits

  • Face the person before speaking.
  • Get attention first.
  • Do not speak from another room.
  • Speak clearly, not loudly.
  • Reduce background noise when possible.
  • Rephrase instead of saying "never mind."
  • Speak one at a time.
  • Write down important details.
  • Support hearing aid use without criticism.
